Work Prints About

Not for anyone. Not for anything.
Not for reward, not even to be found.
Me and my thoughts, quiet and unhurried.

My photographs are small mirrors I leave lying around:
My thoughts, my character, my unspoken desires.
This is who I am, and who I’m walking toward.
The change I see, the change I want to be.

No message. No story.
No rules, no culture, no tradition.
No one has to force me, not even myself.

Untethered, in the light.

Raju Mandal

A visual artist dedicated to the exploration of negative space, fleeting light, and quiet tension. The philosophy of the studio is rooted in curated restraint—allowing the absence of visual noise to amplify the emotional gravity of the subject.

Through asymmetrical composition and paced visual storytelling, each photograph becomes an invitation to pause, reflect, and find the subtle fires burning within the dark.
